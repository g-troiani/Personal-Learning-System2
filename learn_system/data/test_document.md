## Key Concepts

### Retrieval Practice

Retrieval practice is the act of trying to recall information from memory.
It strengthens memory more effectively than passive review.

### Spaced Repetition

Spacing out practice over time leads to better long-term retention than
massed practice (cramming).

### Interleaving

Mixing different topics during practice improves the ability to discriminate
between concepts and transfer knowledge to new situations.

## Summary

These three techniques form the foundation of effective learning:
1. Test yourself frequently (retrieval practice)
2. Space out your study sessions (spaced repetition)
3. Mix up your practice (interleaving)
